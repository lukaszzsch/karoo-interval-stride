# Paused workout

- **You pause** (pause button, top right): a yellow box "Workout paused" replaces "Interval x / y",
  left of the time, sized to its text. Its pause icon blinks about once a second.
- The pause button becomes a large **green half-pill resume button**, rounded on the left and flat
  against the screen edge, with a filled play symbol. It pulses between two greens in step with the
  blink. Light: dark green + white; dark: bright green + dark green.
- **Karoo pauses the ride** (auto-pause or its own pause): the box says "Workout auto-paused" and the
  resume button is hidden, since the workout resumes with the ride.
- Pausing stops only the interval timer; the ride keeps recording and the lap average keeps counting.
- Yellow `#FAC775` with text `#412402` (about 10:1) in both modes.
